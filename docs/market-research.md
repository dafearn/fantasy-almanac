# Market research: what league sites and fantasy tools offer

October 2026. What the big fantasy sites and the league-history/recap tools do, and what this app takes from each.

## What's out there

| Source | What it does | Taken for this app |
|---|---|---|
| [FantasyPros](https://www.fantasypros.com/2025/07/best-fantasy-football-tools/) | League Analyzer, Draft Analyzer, Trade Analyzer, Waiver Assistant, Boom/Bust, Quality Starts: all built on expert projections | Rebuilt using the league's actual results instead of projections (v1: standings, draft grades, trade grades, boom/bust) |
| [RotoBaller Team Sync](https://www.rotoballer.com/team-sync-custom-tools-and-fantasy-advice?pa=editors) | Imports your league; draft assistant, trade analyzer, free agent finder | Same idea as FantasyPros; nothing new for a history site |
| [Fantasy Genius yearly recaps](https://fantasygenius.substack.com/p/fg-yearly-recaps-now-available) | Record vs. the weekly median, schedule simulator (your record with another team's schedule), All-League and "bush league" lineups, humorous awards, draft bubbles | **Schedule luck page** (schedule swap + median record), **All-League teams** with Bush League |
| [League Rewind](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6499177011) | AI weekly and season recaps, shareable to the group chat | Weekly reports (v1) with copy-as-text |
| [The Sunday Chronicle](https://peerpush.com/p/the-sunday-chronicle) | Multi-platform league almanac: champions, drafts, rivalries, head-to-head, weekly pick'ems | **Pick'em**, named rivalries |
| [Yahoo trophies and medals](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN22671.html) | Trophy case and medals on every manager's profile | **Trophy room** with automatic badges |
| [Sleeper](https://www.fantasylife.com/articles/best-ball/sleeper-fantasy-a-deep-dive) | Trade block (a list of who's available), league chat, DMs | **Trade block with bidding**: Sleeper only lists players; here people bid, compare value and argue in the thread |

## Gaps nobody fills (and this app now does)

- **Auctions on the trade block.** Sleeper's block is a passive list; there's no bidding, no history of offers, and no read on whether an offer is fair.
- **League lore from the league's own numbers.** The Receipts, the Trophy room and the Hall of Fame turn a league's history into running jokes with sources: who left the most on the bench, who wins ugly, who has never beaten whom.
- **Accountability for predictions.** Pick'em against "The Model" (always picks the favorite) shows who actually knows the league.
